# Vendor Emergency Contact Sheet

One page per site, posted in the control room and carried by the shift lead. It answers three questions at 3 a.m.: who do I call, what will they ask me, and how soon will they arrive.

> Keep the completed sheet out of public repositories. It contains contact and contract details.

## What the vendor will ask (have this ready before calling)

- Site name and address as registered with the vendor, and the contract number
- Equipment make, model, serial number, and firmware version
- Asset tag and location
- Alarm codes and messages, exactly as displayed, with photographs
- Current state: what is running, what is not, whether load is at risk
- What has already been tried
- Site access instructions, escort arrangements, and required PPE

## Before a vendor travels

- [ ] Fault information sent so the technician brings the right parts and tools
- [ ] Parts confirmed: on site, in the vendor's vehicle stock, or being shipped, with arrival time
- [ ] Access approved and security told
- [ ] Emergency change ticket opened
- [ ] Site contact named, who will meet and brief the technician on arrival
