# Drill Schedule and Scorecard

A drill rehearses an EOP without causing the event. It tests the procedure as much as the people.

## Drill types

| Type | What happens | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Tabletop | The team talks through a scenario with the EOP and drawings | New EOPs, new staff, complex events |
| Walk-through | The team goes to the equipment and points to each device and indication, operating nothing | Most drills |
| Simulated | An alarm or condition is simulated under a MOP; the team responds for real up to agreed limits | Mature teams, with change approval |

**Drill safety rules:** announce "this is a drill" at the start and on every call. Operate nothing unless the drill has an approved MOP. Stop the drill at once if a real event occurs, and say "drill terminated, real event".
