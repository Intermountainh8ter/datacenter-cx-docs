# Operations Program Overview

Procedures only work inside a program that keeps them current, keeps people able to use them, and keeps the equipment maintained. This page lists the parts of that program and how often each is reviewed. The intervals shown as blanks are set by the facility owner.

## The parts of the program

| Part | What it does | Document in this repository |
|---|---|---|
| Procedures | Control planned, routine, and emergency work | MOP, SOP, and EOP templates |
| Change and work authorization | Stops unapproved or conflicting work | Change and Work Authorization SOP |
| Training and qualification | Makes sure the person doing the task is able and signed off | [Training and Qualification Matrix](training-and-qualification-matrix.md) |
| Drills | Proves the EOPs and the people work under pressure | [Drill Schedule and Scorecard](drill-schedule-and-scorecard.md) |
| Maintenance | Keeps equipment in its commissioned state | [Maintenance Calendar](maintenance-calendar.md) |
| Vendor support | Gets the right help to site fast | [Vendor Emergency Contact Sheet](vendor-emergency-contact-sheet.md) |
| Critical spares | Makes failures recoverable | Spares program guide and register |
| Shift operations | Keeps the true state of the facility known | Rounds SOP, Shift Turnover SOP |
| Learning | Turns events into improvements | Post-Event Review template |

## Review rhythm

| Activity | Suggested rhythm | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Rounds and shift turnover | Every shift | Shift lead |
| Change calendar review | Weekly | Operations lead |
| Open issues, overdue maintenance, spares shortages | Monthly | Facility manager |
| Drill of at least one EOP | Every ____ | Operations lead |
| Vendor contact sheet check (call every number) | Every ____ | Operations lead |
| Spares audit | Every ____ | Spares owner |
| Training matrix review and requalification | Every ____ | Facility manager |
| Procedure review (each SOP and EOP) | Every ____, and after any related event or equipment change | Procedure owner |
| Program review with the owner | Yearly | Facility manager |

## Measures worth tracking

- Maintenance completed on time, and the age of the oldest overdue task
- Changes completed without deviation or backout
- Drills held against plan, and average drill score
- People qualified for each critical task per shift (never fewer than two)
- Critical spares below minimum
- Open post-event actions and their age
- Time at reduced redundancy, planned and unplanned

## Starting from commissioning

The best time to build this program is before turnover. Operations staff attend the functional and integrated tests, draft the EOPs from what they see, and take the commissioning results as their baseline: start times, transfer times, time-to-limit, and normal readings for rounds.
