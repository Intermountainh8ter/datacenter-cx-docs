# Training and Qualification Matrix

Shows who is allowed to do what. A person performs a task alone only when the matrix says they are qualified for it.

## Qualification levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Not trained |
| 1 | Trained: has read the procedure and received instruction. May assist |
| 2 | Qualified: has performed the task under supervision and been signed off. May perform, with a verifier where the procedure requires one |
| 3 | Qualified to supervise and to sign off others |

## How a person moves up

1. Reads the procedure and the related drawings and sequences.
2. Receives instruction at the equipment from a level 3 person.
3. Observes the task.
4. Performs the task under supervision, as many times as the owner requires: ____
5. Is signed off by a level 3 person and the facility manager.
6. Requalifies every ____, after a long absence, and after any change to the equipment or procedure.

Statutory and safety qualifications (for example, electrical safety training for qualified persons) are separate, are set by law and the site safety program, and are prerequisites: [VERIFY: NFPA 70E section on training requirements for qualified persons].

## Vendors and contractors

Vendor technicians are qualified by their employer for their equipment. The site still confirms, before work: evidence of qualification for the exact equipment, a site induction, and a briefing on the site's procedures and emergency arrangements.

## Records

Keep sign-off sheets, training attendance, and requalification dates. Review the matrix whenever staff, equipment, or procedures change.
