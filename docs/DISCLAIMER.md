# Disclaimer

**Read this before using anything in this repository.**

## These are templates and examples, not procedures for your site

Every document here is a generic starting point. None of it is a procedure for any real facility. Before any document is used on real equipment it must be rewritten for the specific site, checked against current drawings and the manufacturer's instructions for the exact equipment installed, and reviewed and approved by qualified persons with authority over that facility.

## Drafts are unverified

Every document is marked `DRAFT — author review pending` until the author changes it. A draft has not been checked for technical accuracy or completeness. The documents were drafted with the help of an AI system and are awaiting the author's review. Only tagged releases are intended to be relied on as reviewed, and even a tagged release remains a template.

## Not professional advice

Nothing here is engineering, safety, legal, regulatory, or insurance advice. Using these documents does not create a professional relationship with the author or any contributor. They do not replace a licensed engineer, a qualified electrical worker, a commissioning authority, the equipment manufacturer, or the authority having jurisdiction.

## Work on critical infrastructure can kill people and destroy equipment

The activities described involve electrical energy, stored energy in batteries, rotating machinery, fuel, pressurized systems, fire suppression agents, and the risk of losing power or cooling to critical loads. Only trained, qualified, and authorized people should perform them, under the site's own safety program, permits, and emergency arrangements. In an emergency, protect people first and follow the site's emergency plan and the instructions of the emergency services.

## Standards, codes, and manufacturer instructions come first

These documents do not reproduce or interpret any standard or code. Where a requirement or a limit comes from a standard, the document shows a `[VERIFY: ...]` placeholder and gives no value. Always work from the current edition of the applicable codes and standards, local law, the project's design basis, and the manufacturer's instructions. Where any of those differ from a document here, they govern.

Names of standards bodies and standards are used only to identify references. This repository is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by any standards body, manufacturer, or regulator.
