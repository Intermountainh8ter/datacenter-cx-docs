# EOP: Fire Alarm or Suppression Discharge in a Critical Space

> Example EOP. The site fire safety plan, the local fire service, and the fire system designer's sequence govern. Life safety comes before equipment and before uptime.

## 1. Document control
Use the block from the [EOP Template](eop-template.md).

## 2. Entry conditions
Fire alarm, pre-discharge alarm, suppression discharge, or sight or smell of smoke in an electrical room, battery room, data hall, or plant room.

## 3. Pre-site plan (preparedness)
- [ ] Site fire safety plan current, with evacuation routes and assembly points posted
- [ ] Fire system sequence known by operators: detection stages, timers, abort and manual release functions, which equipment shuts down automatically
- [ ] Fire system inspected and tested on its required cycle: [VERIFY: NFPA 72 sections on inspection, testing, and maintenance]
- [ ] For clean-agent or gas systems, re-entry rules posted: [VERIFY: NFPA 2001 section on personnel safety and re-entry after discharge]
- [ ] Fire service pre-incident information prepared: site plan, hazards (batteries, fuel, energized equipment), contacts, and where to meet responders
- [ ] Fire system vendor emergency contact current; agent recharge arrangements known
- [ ] Portable extinguishers of the correct type in place; staff trained
- [ ] Drills held on the site's cycle

## 4. Immediate actions

| # | Action | Expected response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Raise the alarm if it has not sounded; call the fire service per the site plan | Alarm active; call made |
| 2 | Evacuate the affected space; close doors behind you | Space clear |
| 3 | Account for everyone, including vendors and visitors | Headcount complete |
| 4 | Do not re-enter. Meet the fire service and hand over the pre-incident information | Responders briefed |
| 5 | Notify the shift lead and facility manager | Acknowledged |

Use an extinguisher only if trained, the fire is small, and the exit is behind you. Use the abort function only as the site fire plan allows.

## 5. Follow-up actions

| # | Action | Expected response | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | From outside the space, check the fire panel: zone, device, stage | Location known | |
| 2 | From the monitoring system, check what shut down automatically (air handling, dampers, power) | Status known | |
| 3 | Check redundant systems are carrying the load | Confirmed or escalated | |
| 4 | Watch temperatures in the affected and adjacent spaces remotely | Trend known | |
| 5 | If the fire service asks for equipment to be de-energized, the shift lead directs it using the one-line and tells the load owners | De-energized as requested | |
| 6 | Keep a log of times, actions, and instructions received | Log current | |

## 6. Escalation and communication

| When | Who is told | By whom |
|---|---|---|
| At once | Fire service, shift lead, facility manager | Operator |
| Confirmed fire or discharge | Owner, load owners, fire system vendor, insurer as required | Facility manager |

## 7. Do not
- Do not re-enter until the fire service or the person in charge releases the space.
- Do not silence or reset the fire panel before the cause is found and the responders agree.
- Do not restart air handling into a space after a discharge until re-entry rules are met.
- Do not fight a battery or electrical fire beyond your training.

## 8. Backout procedure (return to normal)
- **Conditions:** space released by the fire service; cause found; atmosphere safe for re-entry; fire system vendor on site or consulted.
- **Steps:** inspect equipment before re-energizing anything that shut down or was exposed to smoke, agent, or water; restore power and cooling under MOPs; fire system vendor resets the panel, replaces used detectors or releases, and recharges agent.
- **While the fire system is impaired:** run a fire watch as the site plan and insurer require, and restrict hot work.
- **Verification:** fire panel normal with no troubles, suppression charged and armed, dampers and shutdowns reset, normal lineup, redundancy restated.

## 9. After the event
- [ ] Fire system vendor report received
- [ ] Equipment exposed to smoke or agent assessed by its manufacturer
- [ ] Post-event review held; fire plan and this EOP revised as needed
