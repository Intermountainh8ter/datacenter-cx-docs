## 2. Entry conditions
The alarms or observations that mean this EOP applies.

## 3. Pre-site plan (preparedness)
An emergency cannot be planned for a date, so the pre-site plan is what must already be in place:
- [ ] EOP posted at the equipment and in the control room
- [ ] Staff trained and drilled on this EOP within the site's drill cycle
- [ ] Emergency contacts current (utility, vendors, fuel, owner, fire service)
- [ ] Vendor emergency response terms agreed, including how a responding vendor is briefed before arriving on site
- [ ] Critical spares for this event in stock (see the spares program)
- [ ] Tools, PPE, portable equipment, and keys staged and checked
