# Spare Parts Criticality Scoring Worksheet

> The scales, weights, and thresholds below are an example starting point, not a standard. The facility owner sets the final values.

## Scales (score each factor 1 to 5)

| Score | Impact if the part fails | Redundancy cover | Lead time to site | Failure likelihood | Fit on site |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No effect on critical systems | Fully covered, no time limit | Same day | Rare, no wear | Staff fit it quickly |
| 2 | Nuisance; monitoring only | Covered; long acceptable time | Next day | Uncommon | Staff fit it with a procedure |
| 3 | One unit lost; redundancy reduced | Covered; limited acceptable time | Several days | Occasional; known wear item | Vendor needed; arrives fast |
| 4 | Redundancy lost for a system | Covered only at reduced load | Weeks | Frequent or aging population | Vendor needed; slow to arrive |
| 5 | Load lost or at immediate risk | Not covered | Months, or part obsolete | Known weak point | Specialist with long mobilization |

## Example scoring method

Criticality score = (Impact x 2) + Redundancy cover + (Lead time x 2) + Failure likelihood + Fit on site

Impact and lead time are doubled in this example because together they decide how long the facility is exposed.

## Example decision bands (owner to set)

| Score | Decision |
|---|---|
| High band | Stock on site |
| Middle band | Stock on site, or vendor-held with a written delivery commitment |
| Low band | Order on failure |

**Override rule:** any part with Impact 5, or with a lead time longer than the acceptable time at reduced redundancy, is stocked regardless of total score.
