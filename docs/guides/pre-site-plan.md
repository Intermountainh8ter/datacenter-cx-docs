# Pre-Site Plan Template

The pre-site plan is everything that must be true **before anyone travels to site or the change window opens**. Most failed maintenance windows fail here: a missing part, an expired calibration, an approval nobody chased. Complete this plan, then attach it to the MOP, SOP, Cx script, or IST script it supports.

## 1. Scope confirmation
- [ ] Scope of work agreed in writing with the facility owner
- [ ] Equipment identified by asset tag, verified against current one-line and as-built drawings
- [ ] Systems affected and systems at reduced redundancy listed
- [ ] What is explicitly out of scope listed

## 2. Approvals and notifications
- [ ] Change ticket submitted and approved at the required risk level
- [ ] Window does not conflict with other work, freeze periods, or utility notices
- [ ] Stakeholders notified (operations, customers or tenants, security, monitoring center)
- [ ] Vendor and subcontractor attendance confirmed in writing

## 3. Documents reviewed
- [ ] Current revision of the procedure, reviewed by a second qualified person
- [ ] Manufacturer instructions for the exact model and firmware
- [ ] One-lines, sequences of operation, and protective device settings
- [ ] Previous reports for the same equipment (last maintenance, open issues)
- [ ] Applicable emergency procedures identified and on hand

## 4. People
- [ ] Named roles filled: supervisor, performer, verifier, safety lead, communicator
- [ ] Qualifications and site access current for every person
- [ ] Rest and shift length reasonable for the window; relief identified for long windows

## 5. Tools, test equipment, and parts
- [ ] Tool list complete; insulated tools and PPE inspected
- [ ] Test equipment in calibration, certificates on hand
- [ ] Parts received, inspected, and matched to the installed model and revision
- [ ] Critical spares for the worst credible failure confirmed in stock (see the spares program guide)
- [ ] Temporary equipment (load banks, cables, cooling) sized, scheduled, and inspected

## 6. Risk review
- [ ] Worst credible outcome stated, with the response
- [ ] Single points of failure during the work identified
- [ ] Hold points and the point of no return marked in the procedure
- [ ] Backout procedure reviewed, with the time it needs compared to the window
- [ ] External conditions checked: weather, utility stability, other site activity

## 7. Site logistics
- [ ] Access, escorts, permits, and delivery arrangements confirmed
- [ ] Laydown area, lighting, and communications coverage confirmed
- [ ] Waste, spill, and housekeeping plan agreed

## 8. Go/no-go
All boxes above are checked, or each exception is accepted in writing by the approver.
