## 2. Purpose and scope
What routine task this covers, on which systems, and who may perform it.

## 3. Risk
State what could go wrong during this routine task and which EOP applies. An SOP must never reduce redundancy. If the task does, it needs a MOP.

## 4. Pre-site plan
For routine tasks the pre-site plan is the standing preparation, confirmed before each use:
- [ ] Current SOP revision in hand
- [ ] Person assigned is trained and signed off on this SOP
- [ ] Tools, PPE, and consumables available
- [ ] No conflicting work or open change windows on the same system
- [ ] For vendor-performed tasks: access, escort, and scope confirmed before travel

## 5. Safety
Hazards, PPE, and limits of the task. What the performer must not do under this SOP.
