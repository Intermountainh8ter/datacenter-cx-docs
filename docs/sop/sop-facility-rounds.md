## 2. Purpose and scope
A walking inspection of critical infrastructure to catch problems that monitoring does not: leaks, noise, smell, heat, damage, and housekeeping. Rounds are look, listen, and record. No switching or adjustment is done under this SOP.

## 3. Risk
Low. Hazards are those of the spaces entered. A finding may trigger an EOP.

## 4. Pre-site plan
- [ ] Current rounds sheet and route in hand
- [ ] Previous shift's rounds and turnover notes read
- [ ] Open change windows and work in progress known
- [ ] Radio or phone, flashlight, hearing protection, and thermal imager or temperature gun (if used) carried
- [ ] Monitoring center knows rounds are starting

## 5. Safety
Hearing protection in generator and plant rooms. Do not open energized enclosures. Do not enter battery or fuel spaces if the gas or leak alarm is active; follow the EOP.

## 7. Abnormal conditions
- Reading outside normal but stable: record, notify the shift lead, raise a ticket.
- Reading trending the wrong way or an active alarm: notify the shift lead at once.
- Leak, smoke, burning smell, or loss of a system: go to the matching EOP.
- Breaker or valve not in its normal position: do not move it. Report it.

## 8. Backout procedure
Rounds change nothing, so backout means stopping safely.

| If stopped at | Do this | End state |
|---|---|---|
| Any point | Close doors and panels opened, tell the monitoring center where the rounds stopped and why, and note which areas were not checked for the next shift | No change to systems; gap recorded |

## 9. Records
Rounds sheet signed and filed each shift. The shift lead reviews trends weekly.
